Mental Health Resources

Mental health is a vital part of overall well-being, especially for women managing chronic conditions like PCOS, Endometriosis, or hormonal imbalances. This page provides supportive resources, tips, and links to help you care for your mind as well as your body.

Why Mental Health Matters

Chronic health conditions can increase stress, anxiety, and emotional challenges. Taking care of your mental health can improve your quality of life and help you manage physical symptoms more effectively.

Self-Care & Coping Tips

  • Practice regular self-care: rest, hobbies, and time with loved ones.
  • Maintain a healthy sleep schedule.
  • Stay physically active—gentle exercise can boost mood.
  • Keep a journal to process emotions.
  • Set realistic goals and celebrate small wins.

Meditation & Mindfulness

  • Calm App – Guided meditations and sleep stories
  • Headspace – Mindfulness and meditation for stress relief
  • Yoga with Adriene – Free yoga and mindfulness videos

Therapy & Counseling

  • BetterHelp – Online therapy with licensed professionals
  • Talkspace – Text, video, and audio therapy
  • Ask your doctor for a referral to a local therapist or counselor
